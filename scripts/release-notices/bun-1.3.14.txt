components and their licenses:

  pinned by Bun 1.3.14 is `5488984d20e0dbfe4be2c3ba8fb18eb81a5e0e8b`, as
  declared by `scripts/build/deps/webkit.ts` at that tag.
- BoringSSL: multiple licenses; see its upstream LICENSE.
- Brotli, ls-hpack, ls-qpack, mimalloc, c-ares, libdeflate, uucode: MIT.
- libarchive: multiple licenses; see upstream COPYING.
- lol-html: BSD 3-Clause.

- picohttp: Perl License or MIT.
- zstd: BSD or GPL-2.0.

- zlib-ng: zlib license.
- libicu: ICU license.
- libbase64 and libspng: BSD 2-Clause.
- libjpeg-turbo: BSD 3-Clause, IJG, and zlib licenses.

- tinycc: LGPL-2.1.

### LGPL relinking

LocalBase is compiled with Bun and statically includes JavaScriptCore. Under
the LGPL-2 relinking terms described by Bun, the complete corresponding
LocalBase source is available in the LocalBase repository under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
Users can modify JavaScriptCore and rebuild/relink the application using that
source. Bun's LGPL information and rebuild instructions are at
https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/blob/bun-v1.3.14/LICENSE.md.
