## Unicode data provenance

The pinned uucode subtree refers to `LICENSE_unicode`, but that file is absent
from the pinned Bun tree. The checked-in uucode license supplies only Jacob
Sandlund's 2025 MIT notice. The separately shipped ICU-72 notice is for ICU's
Unicode 15.0 data and does not establish the revision or terms of uucode's
Unicode tables. The table-data notice and revision therefore remain
unreconciled; no Unicode data copyright holder or revision is inferred here.
