## Bun's pinned component inventory

`Bun-LICENSE.txt` is the complete `LICENSE.md` from oven-sh/bun tag `bun-v1.3.14`: https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/blob/bun-v1.3.14/LICENSE.md. It contains Bun's full linked-library and embedded-polyfill inventory, license identifiers, upstream license URLs, and the copyright notices included by that source. The source archive is https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/archive/refs/tags/bun-v1.3.14.tar.gz.

`ICU-72.txt` is from unicode-org/icu release tag `release-72-1`: https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/blob/release-72-1/icu4c/LICENSE.
