# Model licenses

LocalBase **doesn't redistribute model weights**. When you install a model, it downloads from the model's publisher, usually on Hugging Face, and you accept that model's license.

- The catalog shows each model's license, and `local-base models show <id>` prints it, along with a commercial-use summary. That summary is a convenience, not legal advice, so read the license itself.
- Imported models and your own catalog entries show what their source declares. LocalBase can't verify it, and unknown stays unknown.
- Outputs may carry obligations under a model's license. Those are yours to check.
- LocalBase's AGPL license covers LocalBase, not the models it runs or what they generate.
