What will not work
  • Anything that needs /v1/responses, /v1/completions or Anthropic's /v1/messages. LocalBase serves chat completions.
  • A key used from a web page. Browsers are refused on purpose, and there are no CORS headers.
  • A service that calls the address from its own servers. It cannot reach this computer's own address, only a public one.
