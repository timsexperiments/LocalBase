Settings

Chat history

Optional browser storage, visible to others using this browser. Credentials, attachments, and generated media are never saved. Turning this off removes saved history.

Generation controls

Temperature, output length and the settings for images, video and speech are in Chat, under Generation controls next to the message box. They apply until the page reloads and are not saved.
